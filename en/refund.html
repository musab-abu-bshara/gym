Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2025

At FitGym, we want you to be completely satisfied with your membership. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued.

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee:

If you're not satisfied with your membership within the first 7 days, we offer a full refund including the registration fee. This applies to all membership types.

Conditions:

  • Request must be made within 7 days of joining
  • Membership card must be returned
  • No outstanding balances
After 7 Days:

Monthly Memberships:

  • No refunds for partial months
  • Must provide 30 days' notice to cancel
  • Registration fee is non-refundable after 7 days

Quarterly Memberships (3 Months):

  • Prorated refund available after 7 days, minus 20% administrative fee
  • Minimum usage of 1 month before refund eligibility
  • Registration fee is non-refundable

Annual Memberships (12 Months):

  • Prorated refund available after 7 days, minus 25% administrative fee
  • Minimum usage of 3 months before refund eligibility
  • Registration fee is non-refundable
  • Special promotional rates are non-refundable after 7 days
Medical or Relocation Exceptions:

Full prorated refund (minus 10% administrative fee) available with documentation:

  • Medical certificate preventing exercise for 90+ days
  • Proof of relocation 50+ km from facility
  • Must be submitted within 30 days of circumstance

Unused Class Packages:

Class packages can be refunded within 30 days of purchase if:

  • At least 50% of classes remain unused
  • Refund amount: 50% of remaining class value
  • Valid for 30 days from purchase date only
After 30 Days:
  • No refunds available
  • Classes remain valid until expiration date (typically 6 months)
  • Cannot be transferred to another person
Class Cancellations by FitGym:

If we cancel a class you've booked:

  • Full credit to your account
  • Can be used for any other class
  • No expiration on credits from cancelled classes

Session Cancellation Policy:

By Member:

  • 24+ hours notice: Full credit, session retained
  • Less than 24 hours: 50% charge applied
  • No-show: Full session charged, no refund

By Trainer:

  • Full credit to your account
  • Option to reschedule or refund
Package Refunds:
  • Within 7 days of purchase: Full refund if no sessions used
  • After 7 days: Prorated refund for unused sessions minus 15% fee
  • Must have completed at least one session before package refund
  • Discounted package rates are forfeited; refund based on regular single-session price
Session Validity:
  • Personal training packages expire 6 months from purchase
  • No refunds for expired sessions
  • Extensions available with trainer approval for medical reasons

14-Day Return Policy:

Merchandise purchased at FitGym can be returned within 14 days:

  • Items must be unused and in original packaging
  • Tags must be attached
  • Original receipt required
  • Full refund or store credit available
Non-Returnable Items:
  • Supplements and nutritional products (unless sealed and unopened)
  • Personal hygiene items
  • Sale/clearance items (final sale)
  • Custom-ordered products
Defective Products:

Defective items can be exchanged or refunded at any time with proof of purchase.

Refund Request Process:

Step 1: Submit Request

  • Email: refunds@fitgym.com
  • In-person: Visit front desk with ID and membership card
  • Phone: +966 50 123 4567

Step 2: Required Information

  • Full name and membership number
  • Reason for refund request
  • Original payment method details
  • Supporting documentation (if applicable)

Step 3: Processing Time

  • Refund requests reviewed within 5 business days
  • Approval notification sent via email
  • Refunds processed within 7-14 business days
  • Credited to original payment method
Contact Information:
  • Refunds Email: refunds@fitgym.com
  • Phone: +966 50 123 4567
  • Hours: Mon-Fri, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: Refunded to same card (7-14 business days)
  • Bank Transfer: Refunded to original account (5-10 business days)
  • Cash Payment: Cash refund available at front desk or bank transfer

If original payment method is no longer available, alternative arrangements can be made through our finance team.

Extraordinary Situations:

We understand that exceptional circumstances may arise. Cases will be reviewed individually for:

  • Serious injury or long-term illness
  • Job loss or financial hardship
  • Military deployment
  • Pregnancy complications

Please contact our management team at management@fitgym.com with documentation.

Disputes:

If you're not satisfied with a refund decision, you may appeal by contacting our Customer Service Manager within 14 days of the decision.

Note: This refund policy is subject to change. Members will be notified of any updates. For questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
